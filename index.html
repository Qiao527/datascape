The Hidden Dimension

Datascape

Every chart here is flat because something was folded away: height, density, time, the ground under the water. Each piece starts from a chart you have seen before, built from real measurements, and opens it back into the space it came from.

Stories Guided, in chapters, from public scientific data

  • Weather radar map → the storm it was measured on

    Storm Chamber

    One volume scan of the Dodge City radar, thirteen minutes before the 2013 Rozel tornado: fourteen cones of measurement, filled into a box of sky you can cut open.

    8 chaptersNOAA NEXRAD Level II

  • Phase diagram → the surface that casts it

    The Shadow of Water

    The textbook diagram of ice, liquid and vapor is a shadow. This is the object that casts it: every state of water in pressure, temperature and density.

    8 chaptersIAPWS-95 via CoolProp

  • 12-lead ECG → the loop of the heart's vector

    The Electric Heart

    Twelve traces on red paper are projections of one moving point. Fold time away and each heartbeat becomes a loop in space, ten beats from a healthy heart.

    8 chaptersPTB Diagnostic ECG, PhysioNet

  • Tide gauge curve → the shore it floods

    Between Tides

    A month of measured sea level at Eastport, Maine, carried sideways onto the surveyed ground of the South Lubec flats, where seven meters of tide uncover square kilometers of seabed.

    8 chaptersNOAA CO-OPS · CUDEM

Instruments Bring your own data: a GitHub account, a song

  • GitHub contribution calendar → a city at night

    Commit City

    Type any GitHub username and a year of contributions rises as a city, one lit building a day, or as a glowing contour terrain.

  • Stacked pulsar plot → ridges driven by music

    PSR B1919+21

    A tribute to the most famous plot in radio astronomy. Play a song, open the microphone or start the built-in synth, and its spectrum flows away as white ridges.

    InteractiveYour music
